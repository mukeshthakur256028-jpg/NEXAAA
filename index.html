Can I pay cash on delivery?

COD is a planned payment option for eligible Indian pincodes and order values. Availability and any COD fee must be confirmed before an order is placed. This storefront is a preview; no live orders or payments are accepted.

Where do you deliver, and how long does it take?

The store is being designed for delivery within India. Final serviceability, shipping charges, and a product-specific delivery estimate will be provided before payment. Items fulfilled by different suppliers may arrive separately. See our .

What if a product isn’t quite right?

The proposed launch policy allows return requests within 7 days of delivery for eligible, unused items in their original packaging. Product-specific exclusions must be disclosed before purchase. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items are handled separately. Read the .

How will payments be protected?

At launch, online payments should be processed through a supported payment provider with UPI, cards, and netbanking. We will not ask for your UPI PIN, OTP, or complete card details over chat. No payment gateway is connected in this preview, and no payment information is collected.

Are the product reviews verified?

There are no published reviews in this preview. A review should only be labelled “verified purchase” when it can be matched to a genuine order. The review form currently saves a private draft on your device, not a public review.
